We discover a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside LLMs that separates truthful and hallucinatory representations. This finding provides new insights into the internal mechanisms of language models and opens up possibilities for controlling their truthfulness. The associated code is available in the [Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane) repository.
