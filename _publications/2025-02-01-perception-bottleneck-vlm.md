This work investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) when applied to chart understanding tasks. We analyze where current VLMs struggle when interpreting charts and provide insights toward improving their visual perception capabilities. The associated code is available in the [Vision4Chart](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart) repository.
